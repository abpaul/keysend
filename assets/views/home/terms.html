    What you get

    KeySend is a web application at keysend.app that reads your Google Ads every day and tells you what changed, why, and what to do: a daily Update for each Ad Account, a Weekly email, an Alert when something breaks, and an Export as a PDF file.

    The Trial lasts 14 days and needs no payment card. After the Trial, KeySend stops reading your Ad Accounts until you start the Subscription.

    Your account

    You sign in with Google. Each Workspace has one User.

    You're responsible for the Google logins you connect. You must have the right to access each Google Ads account you add, for example a client's permission.

    What KeySend does in Google Ads

    KeySend only reads. It never changes a campaign, an ad, a budget, or a billing setting.

    Google's permission is broader than what KeySend uses, because Google Ads offers no read-only permission. If you want a hard limit, connect a Google login that has Read-only access to the account.

    Updates and Recommendations

    Code calculates every Metric from your Google Ads data. The Metrics depend on the data Google provides.
